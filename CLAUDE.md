# Mission Control: Point-in-Time S&P 500 Net Equity Issuance

You are executing an autonomous, time-bounded financial data engineering mission. Your goal is to build a **true point-in-time (bi-temporal)** dataset, multi-methodology verification analysis, publication charts, and coverage report of **S&P 500 net equity issuance** strictly from primary sources—starting with the trailing 12 months up to today, completing 2021–present as your first multi-year milestone (aligning with Federal Reserve Z.1 and Acadian benchmarks), and then continuing backward year-by-year as far as time permits before `historical_cutoff_min`, architected to keep running incrementally as new filings and announcements are published.

## Non-Negotiable Invariants

1. **Primary Sources Only for Output Numbers**: Every value in `output/events.csv` and every membership boundary must come from primary sources: SEC filings (`10-K`, `10-Q`, `8-K`, `424B*`, `S-3`, `S-4`, `SC TO-I`, `Form 25`, `N-PORT-P`, `N-Q`/`N-CSR`, and their XBRL/iXBRL/XML data), company announcements, or S&P Dow Jones Indices announcements. Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Wikipedia, financial portals, and Acadian are **strictly check-only verification tools**—never copy output figures or membership dates from them.
2. **True Point-in-Time (Bi-Temporal) Event Chains**: Record each offering, buyback, and corporate action from its filing or announcement when published (`published_at`, using SEC `acceptanceDateTime` UTC to the second where available), with the figure as known then. Link initial announcements (`424B`/`8-K`/`SC TO-I`), settlements/amendments, and `10-Q`/`10-K` confirmations or revisions under a shared `event_key` (`is_revision = true`, `supersedes_record_id`, first-class `superseded_at`), keeping ALL prior rows intact so a client querying any historical `as_of_date` sees exactly what was known on that date.
3. **Complete Buyback & Issuance Category Coverage**: Systematically extract and classify all required common-stock categories—Buybacks (`buyback_open_market`, `buyback_asr`, `buyback_tender_offer`), Issuance (`issuance_underwritten_offering`, `issuance_atm_offering`, `issuance_acquisition`, `issuance_employee_equity`, `issuance_warrant_exercise`, `issuance_conversion`), and separately tagged supplemental items (`tax_withholding_net_settlement`, `retirement_cash_merger`)—minimizing vague `unspecified`/`other_common` buckets.
4. **Always-Shippable, Atomically Validated `output/`**: Produce a complete, validated `output/` early (Milestone 1) and atomically refresh it via `work/staging_output/` after every phase and batch. Never leave `output/` partial or unvalidated.
5. **Time Budget, Clock Discipline, Telemetry & Autonomous Continuation**:
   - Read optional `RUN_BUDGET_MINUTES` env var (default `240`). Compute proportional gates at Phase 0 (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: `240m` → Phase 6 at `210m`, Freeze at `225m`; `70m` → Phase 6 at `55m`, Freeze at `65m`; `30m` → Phase 6 at `23m`, Freeze at `28m`).
   - Record wall-clock start/end/duration and item counts for **every phase (0–6)** and batch in the **main** SQLite database's `phase_timings` table (never solely in a scratch DB) and summarize in `work/STATE.md` and `output/COVERAGE.md`.
   - **Assume a hard cutoff at any moment.** The run may stop at the budget (or earlier) without warning. Order all work strictly by value per minute so that whenever it stops, the highest-value work is already published: refresh `output/` after every batch, keep `work/STATE.md` current, and process the largest T12M issuers/rows first.
   - **Use the full budget — work until `freeze_cutoff_min`, no exceptions.** Never pause to ask for user confirmation, and never write a final summary or end the turn while `elapsed < freeze_cutoff_min`. If less time remains than a full batch, run a smaller bounded increment (one Tier 3 filing check, one verifier dispatch, one doc section). *"Too little time left"* is never a reason to stop.
   - **Read the clock, never estimate it.** Every pipeline module MUST print `elapsed=X.X / <budget> min (phase6=<P>, freeze=<F>)` from `work/run_meta.json` at exit; base every routing decision strictly on that printed wall-clock value, never on a mental estimate.
   - **Phase 6 entry is an explicit exit-artifact checklist, not a judgment call.** Enter Phase 6 ONLY when every Phase 1–5 exit artifact exists (checklist in Resume Protocol below) OR `elapsed >= phase6_cutoff_min`. Passing Milestone 1 or the early QA pass is never a reason to jump to Phase 6.
   - **Background agents launch on fixed triggers, never by judgment — never skip or defer one "to save time"** (they cost the main thread almost nothing). Launch each trigger pair in a single message:
     - Phase 1 start → `membership-researcher` + `benchmark-researcher`.
     - Milestone 1 published → `qa-auditor` (early pass) **AND** `market-verifier` (top 25 T12M movers, S&P DJI quarterly buyback totals, mega-IPOs, splits).
     - Every later publish while a verifier is idle → re-dispatch it on the delta (next 25 movers / changed rows).
     - Phase 6 → final `qa-auditor`.
     - Re-task finished background subagents immediately if work remains. **Permission-denial rule**: if any launch or tool call is denied by permissions, log the exact denial in `COVERAGE.md` and move on — **never retry a denied action through another tool or process**.
   - **Fail-Fast Circuit Breaker**: Never spend > 3 retries or > 3 minutes fighting a single blocked external URL or malformed filing; log it, use a legitimate primary source, and keep moving.
6. **SEC EDGAR Fair-Access & Privacy**: Send `$SEC_USER_AGENT` **only** to `sec.gov` hosts (use a clean neutral bot User-Agent without personal email for non-SEC hosts). Enforce a **shared cross-process rate limit ≤ 8 req/sec** across main scripts and subagents via a single gzip-cached client in `work/src/`. Never fetch the same URL twice—**with one controlled freshness exception**: allow a single refresh of `companyfacts` or `submissions` for an active constituent when its latest periodic accession in `submissions` is missing from cached `companyfacts` (or during `--mode incremental`).
7. **Context Hygiene, Engineering Journal & Command Portability**: Never name Python modules after stdlib packages (e.g., use `edgar_http.py`, never `http.py`). Run Python unbuffered as modules (`work/.venv/bin/python -u -m <pkg>.<mod>`, avoiding compound `cd ... && ...` chains and `sleep`). Write verbose logs, parser diagnostics, and large tables to `work/logs/` or SQLite; keep terminal stdout concise (≤ ~25–30 lines per command). Maintain an append-only **`work/logs/challenges_and_issues.md`** journal recording every real data pitfall, parser failure, permission denial, reconciliation surprise, root cause, and fix as it occurs—and distill it into `output/COVERAGE.md`.

## Required Deliverables (in `output/`)

3. `output/net_equity_issuance.svg` & `.png` — Publication-grade S&P 500 net equity issuance & Fed Z.1 comparison chart (matching clean editorial quarterly bar design) with **major IPO events (at minimum SpaceX and Anthropic) directly annotated from primary `424B4` cover pages**.
4. `output/acadian_comparison.svg` & `.png` — Multi-methodology verification graphic comparing cash-flow basis, SBC-fair-value basis, Acadian Eq. (1) total share-count × price (incl. full Magnificent Seven), Acadian float-adjusted basis, and Baker-Wurgler equity share `E/(E+D)`, with SpaceX and Anthropic IPO callouts.
5. `output/COVERAGE.md` — Thorough accounting of coverage vs. uncovered gaps, PIT membership & Wikipedia check, all reconciliation & web-verification checks, timing breakdown, continuous-run verification, and an honest assessment of what may be wrong.

1. Read `elapsed` from `work/run_meta.json` (`start_time_utc`) and load gates (`phase6_cutoff_min`, `freeze_cutoff_min`). Never estimate elapsed time mentally.
2. Read `work/STATE.md` and query `pit_equity.sqlite` for exact progress, pending `work/staging_membership/*.json` files, and last publish timestamp.
3. Route strictly by clock:
   - `elapsed < phase6_cutoff_min` and Phase 6 incomplete → resume next unfinished step immediately. **Phase 6 exit-artifact checklist** — ALL four must exist to enter Phase 6 before `phase6_cutoff_min`:
     - staged membership integrated back to at least 2021;
     - Tier 2 rows (monthly buybacks + `424B`/`8-K`/`SC TO-I` transactions) for at least the top T12M movers;
     - share-count reconciliation results in `qa_checks`;
     - `work/staging_benchmarks/market_verification.json` plus the `benchmark-researcher` files (or their logged permission denial).
   - `elapsed >= phase6_cutoff_min` and Phase 6 incomplete → jump immediately to **Phase 6**.
   - **Phase 6 complete & `elapsed < freeze_cutoff_min` (Safe Incremental Value-Add Loop — Use the Full Budget!)**: Keep looping until `elapsed >= freeze_cutoff_min` — never declare finished earlier, however little time remains. First re-task any idle background agent.
