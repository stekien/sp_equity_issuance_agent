# Mission Control: Point-in-Time S&P 500 Net Equity Issuance

You are executing an autonomous, time-bounded financial data engineering mission. Your goal is to build a **true point-in-time (bi-temporal)** dataset, multi-methodology verification analysis, publication charts, and coverage report of **S&P 500 net equity issuance** strictly from primary sources—starting with the trailing 12 months up to today, completing 2021–present as your first multi-year milestone (aligning with Federal Reserve Z.1 and Acadian benchmarks), and then continuing backward year-by-year as far as time permits before `historical_cutoff_min`, architected to keep running incrementally as new filings and announcements are published.

## Non-Negotiable Invariants

1. **Primary Sources Only for Output Numbers**: Every value in `output/events.csv` and every membership boundary must come from primary sources: SEC filings (`10-K`, `10-Q`, `8-K`, `424B*`, `S-3`, `S-4`, `SC TO-I`, `Form 25`, `N-PORT-P`, and their XBRL/iXBRL/XML data), company announcements, or S&P Dow Jones Indices announcements. Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Wikipedia, financial portals, and Acadian are **strictly check-only verification tools**—never copy output figures or membership dates from them.
2. **True Point-in-Time (Bi-Temporal) Event Chains**: Record each offering, buyback, and corporate action from its filing or announcement when published (`published_at`, using SEC `acceptanceDateTime` UTC to the second where available), with the figure as known then. Link initial announcements (`424B`/`8-K`/`SC TO-I`), settlements/amendments, and `10-Q`/`10-K` confirmations or revisions under a shared `event_key` (`is_revision = true`, `supersedes_record_id`), keeping ALL prior rows intact so a client querying any historical `as_of_date` sees exactly what was known on that date.
3. **Complete Buyback & Issuance Category Coverage**: Systematically extract and classify all required common-stock categories—Buybacks (`buyback_open_market`, `buyback_asr`, `buyback_tender_offer`), Issuance (`issuance_underwritten_offering`, `issuance_atm_offering`, `issuance_acquisition`, `issuance_employee_equity`, `issuance_warrant_exercise`, `issuance_conversion`), and separately tagged supplemental items (`tax_withholding_net_settlement`, `retirement_cash_merger`)—minimizing vague `unspecified`/`other_common` buckets.
4. **Always-Shippable, Atomically Validated `output/`**: Produce a complete, validated `output/` early (Milestone 1) and atomically refresh it via `work/staging_output/` after every phase and batch. Never leave `output/` partial or unvalidated.
5. **Time Budget, Telemetry & Autonomous Continuation**:
   - Read optional `RUN_BUDGET_MINUTES` env var (default `240`). Compute proportional gates at Phase 0 (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: for `240` min → Phase 6 hard gate at `210` min, Freeze gate at `225` min; for `30` min → Phase 6 at `23` min, Freeze at `28` min).
   - Record wall-clock start/end/duration and item counts for **every phase (0–6)** and batch in the **main** SQLite database's `phase_timings` table (never solely in a scratch DB) and summarize in `work/STATE.md` and `output/COVERAGE.md`.
   - **Never pause to ask for user confirmation between phases, and NEVER stop early before `freeze_cutoff_min` while gaps remain.** Do NOT jump to Phase 6 early just because Milestone 1 passed — only enter Phase 6 once Phases 1–5 (historical membership back to 2021, Tier 2 monthly buyback & offering parsers, share-count reconciliation, and `market-verifier`) are genuinely complete OR `elapsed >= phase6_cutoff_min`. Re-task finished background subagents immediately if work remains.
   - **Fail-Fast Circuit Breaker**: Never spend > 3 retries or > 3 minutes fighting a single blocked external URL or malformed filing; log it, use the documented fallback, and keep moving.
6. **SEC EDGAR Fair-Access & Privacy**: Send `$SEC_USER_AGENT` **only** to `sec.gov` hosts (use a clean neutral bot User-Agent without personal email for non-SEC hosts). Enforce a **shared cross-process rate limit ≤ 8 req/sec** across main scripts and subagents via a single cached client in `work/src/`. Never fetch the same URL twice.
7. **Context Hygiene & Command Portability**: Run Python unbuffered using direct single commands (`work/.venv/bin/python -u work/src/...` — avoid compound `cd ... && ...` chains and `sleep` so background subagents match `.claude/settings.json` permissions cleanly). Write verbose logs, parser diagnostics, and large tables to `work/logs/` or SQLite; keep terminal stdout concise (≤ ~25–30 lines per command).
